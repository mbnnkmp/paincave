The four decisions, in order

  1. Where will it live? A spare room, garage or living room changes everything: noise, heat and whether it can stay set up.
  2. Bike or trainer? If you already own a road bike and have under £600, a direct-drive trainer is the answer. Full comparison.
  3. Which app? Try MyWhoosh free first, then pay for Zwift, Rouvy or TrainerRoad if you want what they add.
  4. Cooling, floor and screen. Budget £100 to £200 for a fan, mat and stand. They decide whether you keep riding.
